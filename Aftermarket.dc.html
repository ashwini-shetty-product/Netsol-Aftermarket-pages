Questions, answered

What is a premium or aftermarket domain?
A domain someone already owns and is offering for sale. These names are often short, memorable, or built around popular keywords.
How is buying an aftermarket domain different from registering a new one?
A new registration claims a name nobody has taken, at a standard price. An aftermarket domain is already owned, so you buy it from the current holder and the market sets the price — based on things like length, how long it has been registered, keyword demand, and existing traffic.
Why is this domain more expensive than a regular registration?
You are paying an owner, not just a registration fee. Short, memorable, in-demand names cost more.
Why can I not register this domain right away?
It is not unclaimed — someone owns it. Depending on the listing, you can either buy it instantly or bid for it in an auction.
How do I know whether a domain is a direct purchase or an auction?
The listing tells you. Options vary from one name to the next.
What is the difference between "Purchase" and "Place a bid"?
Purchase — Buy at the listed price, no bidding or negotiation. Fulfillment usually takes under 10 days.
Place a bid — You join a live auction. Bids are binding and cannot be retracted, so if you win, you are committed to buy. If you are outbid, you owe nothing. Auctions take longer, and we will keep you posted at every step.
Am I guaranteed to get the domain if I place a backorder?
No. Others may want the same name, and some factors are outside our control. You will get it only if the domain can be secured and you win the auction.
When will I know if I got the domain?
It depends on the domain and its status — some resolve quickly, others take several days or longer. We will keep you updated at every stage.
What protects me if the seller does not hand over the domain?
You are not charged until the domain is successfully acquired. If it cannot be secured, you pay nothing.
Can I get a refund if I change my mind?
No. Once the domain is acquired and in your account, the sale is final and cannot be cancelled or refunded. Please be sure before you buy or bid.
Am I responsible for checking trademarks and the domain history?
Yes — research any name before you commit. Check the spelling carefully, including letters swapped with numbers (a "1" where you expected an "l"). There are no refunds once a domain is acquired, so if you win a name spelled differently than you intended, or find a trademark on it later, the purchase stands. Confirm the domain does not infringe anyone's rights before you bid or place a backorder.
